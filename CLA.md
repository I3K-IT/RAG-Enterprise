# Contributor Licence Agreement

Thank you for wanting to contribute. Before we can merge a pull request, we
need you to agree to this document. It is short, and this section explains
plainly why it exists.

## Why we ask for this

i3k-rag-engine is released under the **AGPL-3.0**, and i3k also sells a
commercial licence to organisations that cannot accept the AGPL's terms. The
same code additionally forms the base of i3k's proprietary product, i3k RAG
Pro.

That arrangement only works if one party holds the rights to license the whole
codebase both ways. If a contribution arrived under the AGPL alone, i3k could
not include it in a commercially licensed or proprietary build without
breaching your copyright — you would remain its owner, having licensed it to
us under the AGPL only. In practice that would mean either dropping the
contribution or maintaining a permanent record of which lines cannot ship
commercially. Neither is workable.

So this agreement asks you to grant i3k rights broad enough to relicense your
contribution. It does **not** ask you to give up your own rights: see below.

A `Signed-off-by` line (DCO) is not enough for this purpose. The DCO certifies
you have the right to submit the code; it does not grant the right to
relicense it.

## How to sign

Add a line to your pull request description:

```
I have read and agree to the Contributor Licence Agreement in CLA.md.
```

One agreement covers all your future contributions to this project; you do not
need to repeat it on every pull request. If anything here is unclear or a
problem for your situation, open an issue or write to info@i3k.eu before
putting work into a contribution — better to sort it out first than to have a
pull request we cannot merge.
