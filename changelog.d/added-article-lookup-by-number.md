- **A question that names an article by number now gets that article.** Asked "Che cosa prevede l'art. 2875 del codice civile?", vector search returned articles 2475, 2645-bis and 2645-ter — a number carries almost no meaning for bge-m3 — and the model rightly answered that the article was not among its sources. On a 472-question statute exam the article asked was among the sources for 37% of questions. Now the article named in the question (with its code, when the question names one: "c.c.", "c.p.c.", "della Costituzione", "legge n. 241/1990"...) is looked up by file name and placed before the passages found by meaning. It applies to files named after the article they hold, as in the LEGAL_PACK ("Codice civile, art. 2875 - Rubrica.txt"); a library named otherwise answers exactly as before.
